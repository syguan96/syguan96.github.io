About

I am currently a researcher at the vivo Imaging Department, where I work on AI photography R&D. I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2024, advised by Prof. Xiaokang Yang and Prof. Yunbo Wang, and my B.S. from Xidian University in 2017.

Over the years I have interned at Tencent XR, SenseTime, Tencent YouTu Lab, and miHoYo, working across computer vision, AIGC, and gaming.

Research interests: high-resolution image generation, video synthesis, and world models for next-generation computational photography.
